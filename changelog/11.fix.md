Workaround for `scmdata` 0.18.x's incompatibility with pandas 3.0. Two
breakages — `numpy.issubdtype` on a `pandas.StringDtype` column in
`scmdata.groupby`, and positional integer indexing on a label-indexed
`pandas.Series` in `scmdata._xarray._many_to_one` — are now patched in
place when `openscm_runner` is imported. The previously-broken
multi-scenario runs and `NetCDFChunkWriter` writes work cleanly on the
pandas-3 stack. The shim will be removed once a fixed `scmdata` is
released; see fork issue
[#10](https://github.com/benmsanderson/openscm-runner/issues/10).
