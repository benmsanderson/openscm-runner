Added `openscm_runner.scenarios.load_rcmip3_emissions()` for resolving
RCMIP3 protocol scenario IDs against the upstream archives that publish
them. Wires in the RCMIP3 v1.1.7 emissions CSV (gitlab.com/rcmip/rcmip-phase-3:
SSPs, esm-flat* idealised family, historical and single-forcing
attribution runs) and the van Vuuren et al. 2026 ScenarioMIP-CMIP7 CSV
(github.com/benmsanderson/scenariomip-paper-plots: scen7-VL/LN/L/ML/M/H/HL).
Both sources publish emissions in the same IAMC dialect, so the existing
`load_iamc()` harmonisation pipeline is reused unchanged; this module
adds the scenario-id registry, marker-IAM filter for the scen7 source,
on-disk caching under `configurations/rcmip3/`, and the protocol-id
rename so the returned ScmRun's `scenario` column matches the protocol
shorthand (`"ssp245"`, not `"SSP2 - Medium Emissions"`). Table 3 of the
RCMIP3 paper (Romero-Prieto et al. 2025) is also re-exported as
`CONSTRAINT_TARGETS` for downstream evaluation notebooks.
