Added `notebooks/compare_rcmip3.py` — a Jupytext-paired notebook that
reads the netCDF cache written by `scripts/run_rcmip3.py` and produces
five figures: historical-validation against the IGCC2024 / GCB2024 /
AR6 constraint targets from Table 3 of the RCMIP3 paper; per-scenario
SSP and `scen7-*` projection grids with emissions- and
concentration-driven bands overlaid; `esm-flat*` idealised
diagnostics (TCRE convergence + ZEC plateau after year 100); and an
SSP-focused ED-vs-CD comparison panel per model.

The notebook re-runs in <30 s against the cache (no SCM dispatch)
so figure-styling iteration is decoupled from the underlying
~30-40 min sweep. The cache itself lives at `out/rcmip3/` and is
gitignored; regenerate via the runner script.
