- The FaIR, MAGICC7 and CICERO-SCM adapters now respect cluster CPU allocations when sizing their worker pools. The pre-existing per-adapter override env vars (`FAIR_WORKER_NUMBER`, `MAGICC_WORKER_NUMBER`, `CICEROSCM_WORKER_NUMBER`) still take precedence; otherwise the helper falls back to `SLURM_CPUS_PER_TASK`, then `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, then `multiprocessing.cpu_count()` (the pre-existing default). Previously each adapter unconditionally used `cpu_count()` as the fallback, which on a SLURM node reports the whole physical node rather than the `--cpus-per-task` allocation and caused oversubscription when two jobs shared a node. The new helper is exposed as `openscm_runner.settings.get_worker_count`.
