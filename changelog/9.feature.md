Added `openscm_runner.scenarios.load_iamc()` for reading emissions scenario
files in standard IAMC long format. Handles both CSV (RCMIP-style) and Excel
(Scenario Compass Initiative) inputs with the same harmonisation pass:
parent paths (`F-Gases|HFC|`, `HFC|`, `Montreal Gases|`, ...) are stripped
to the flat `Emissions|HFC125` form, legacy species names are renamed
(`SOx` → `Sulfur`, `NMVOC` → `VOC`, `HFC43-10` → `HFC4310mee`), and CO2
sector splits are mapped to MAGICC naming. `Emissions|CO2|AFOLU [NGHGI]`
is dropped (inventory accounting construct, not a physical flux) in favour
of `Emissions|CO2|AFOLU`.
